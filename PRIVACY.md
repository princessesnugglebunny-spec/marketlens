# Privacy Policy — MarketLens

MarketLens runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

- Filtering, profit calculation, buy log, and settings are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your device.
- The extension makes no network requests except: (a) loading its own bundled data files, and (b) requests you explicitly trigger to an OpenAI-compatible API endpoint you configure (your key stays in local storage, never synced).
- It never reads your Facebook messages, never posts, clicks, or changes anything on your account.
- No telemetry, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
